Business examples show how discovery notes can become a structured assistant
brief with goals, boundaries, escalation rules, proposed tools, risks, and
tests.

They are educational designs, not production-ready systems. They do not
configure Hermes, connect a messaging service, or implement business tools.
Every business must review and adapt the logic before relying on it.

Fictional information and placeholders are used so the examples do not expose
real businesses, customers, employees, contact details, prices, credentials,
or operational systems.

{{< warning >}}
Do not replace placeholders in a committed example with production details.
Make a private copy, adapt it with authorised reviewers, and keep customer
records and credentials out of prompts and repository files.
{{< /warning >}}
